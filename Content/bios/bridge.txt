Introducing the methodical Matt 'Bridge' Bainbridge. 
Matt is an experimental archaeologist and engineer who has worked alongside Tom Blackburn for over a decade across many and varied pursuits including running wargaming events and developing their understanding of historical metalworking practices and techniques in their forge. He has performed demonstrations across Anglesey and North Wales, notably at the roundhouses at Melin Llynon Mill and Felin Uchaf.
Over the last year Matt has developed a keen interest in ancient brewing techniques and has been working on his mead brewing process. This ancient honey wine has since become a firm favourite among the Monolith crew for their evening tipple.
Matt joins the team with past experience as a small business owner and mechanical engineer. Whether developing metalworking techniques in the forge, smelting iron from scratch or building equipment such as medieval-inspired bellows, his clear, methodical approach to planning new projects and solving problems is invaluable.
Matt has also developed a reputation for finding the more eccentric holidays for the crew, including their recent trip to Farnhill Castle in Skipton, which brought together a whole group of people from across the world. His curiosity and practical mindset make him just as at home exploring unusual places as he is developing new historical technologies.
Whether he is sitting around the campfire with a mug of mead, tinkering on Monolith's next build or demonstrating ancient metalworking, you can expect to find Matt at upcoming events talking about the skills and technologies of the past. You will also never fail to hear him arriving on site in his classic 1993 Mini Cooper Sport.
