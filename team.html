Our Values

Community First

We center community voices and engagement in everything we do. Archaeology belongs to everyone, and we're committed to removing barriers to participation.

Scientific Excellence

We maintain the highest standards of archaeological practice and scientific methodology while keeping our work accessible and engaging.

Local Heritage

We focus on the archaeology and heritage of North Wales and Anglesey, helping communities understand and celebrate their own local history.

Hands-On Learning

We believe people learn best by doing. Through excavations, experiments, and workshops, we create immersive experiences that bring the past to life.
