Who We Are

Introducing Mônolith Archaeology and Heritage. Mônolith is the culmination of friends and peers who want to remove barriers and make archaeology more accessible to the people and communities of North Wales. The team consists of Suzie whose specialism is working with communities and their connection to their local landscape. Callum an Archaeologist with years of experience excavating throughout the UK and beyond. Dr Mike Woods an archaeologist and specialist in the prehistory of the British Isles, and lastly Tom Blackburn an experimental archaeologist and blacksmith whose work primarily focuses on the metal work of prehistory.

Together we met excavating community digs on Anglesey and have shared a deep passion for making the field of Archaeology and the ability to help communities connect with their local heritage and history more accessible. Mônolith aims to take this further than we have before by opening up more frequent excavations, experiments, demonstrations and talks where the focus is on engagement with people as much as it is with the science of archaeology and heritage.

In 2026 our aim is to bring these experiences to North Wales and will soon be looking for groups of volunteers who want to help us uncover the past.

What We Do

Mônolith makes archaeology accessible and engaging for everyone. We focus on community-led excavations, demonstrations, and educational programs that help people connect with their local heritage and history.

Community-Focused Excavations

We conduct regular archaeological digs that welcome volunteers from all backgrounds. Our excavations on Anglesey and across North Wales are designed to engage communities while advancing our understanding of local prehistory.

Experimental Archaeology

Through hands-on demonstrations and workshops, we bring prehistory to life. From Bronze Age metalworking to Iron Age smelting, participants learn ancient techniques and engage directly with how our ancestors lived and worked.

Heritage Connection Programs

We help communities understand and connect with their local landscape and heritage. Our specialists work with groups to explore the archaeology, history, and stories of their own regions.

Educational Talks & Workshops

We deliver engaging presentations and workshops for schools, community groups, and organizations, making the science and excitement of archaeology accessible to all ages.

Volunteer Opportunities

Looking to get involved? We're actively seeking volunteers to join us on excavations, experiments, and community events across North Wales in 2026 and beyond.

Archaeological Research

Our research focuses on the prehistory of the British Isles, with particular emphasis on the prehistoric landscapes and monuments of North Wales and Anglesey.

Join the Mônolith Community

We're on a mission to make archaeology accessible to everyone in North Wales. Whether you're interested in volunteering on digs, learning ancient skills, or connecting with your local heritage, there's a place for you in our community.
