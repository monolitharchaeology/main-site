Our Partners

We collaborate with leading institutions and organizations committed to archaeological research and heritage preservation.

International Heritage Foundation

A global leader in heritage conservation and archaeological research, supporting projects across six continents.

Global Archaeological Alliance

Connecting researchers and institutions worldwide to advance collaborative archaeological discovery and knowledge sharing.

National Research Institute

Providing scientific expertise and laboratory resources for artifact analysis and conservation work.

UNESCO Regional Office

Partnering on World Heritage site preservation and protection of cultural landscapes.

Community Heritage Networks

Collaborating with local communities to ensure culturally sensitive and socially beneficial archaeological practices.

Academic Publishing Consortium

Supporting the publication and dissemination of archaeological research and findings to the global community.
