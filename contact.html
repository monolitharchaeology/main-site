Frequently Asked Questions

How can I support Monolith's work?

There are several ways to support us: financial donations, volunteering your time and skills, partnerships for projects, or spreading awareness about archaeology and heritage preservation in your networks.

Can I volunteer with Monolith?

We welcome passionate volunteers! Contact us through the form above to inquire about current volunteer opportunities in archaeology, conservation, education, or administration.

How long have you been working in archaeology?

Our team brings together professionals with decades of combined experience in archaeological research, conservation, and heritage preservation across multiple continents.

Where do you conduct archaeological projects?

We work on sites across different regions, collaborating with local communities and institutions. Contact us to learn about our current and upcoming project locations.

How are your artifacts preserved?

We employ international best practices in conservation science, working with certified conservation specialists to ensure proper preservation of all discovered artifacts.

Do you work with universities and research institutions?

Yes! We actively collaborate with academic institutions on research projects, internships, and educational programs. We'd love to discuss partnership opportunities.
